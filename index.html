About my software

I build simple, useful tools for everyday life and work. Two products are available today.

Sortiva — organize your files

Sortiva keeps your folders tidy automatically. Pick a folder and it sorts every file into the right place — images, videos, documents, archives and more — with no ads, no tracking and no cloud.

Sazora — manage construction projects

Sazora is built for construction projects and daily work management — organizing tasks, documents and project details in one clean place.

Why support matters

Both products are free and independent. Your support funds updates, new features and the next applications — while keeping the work ad-free and privacy-first.
